How it works:

The prompt contains a Base64 encoded string: U0VDUkVUX0hPTkVZ, which is "SECRET_HONEY" in Base64. If the LLM is instructed to decode it, it might reveal the hidden token, bypassing any filters that look for the plaintext "SECRET_HONEY".

Mitigation:

Detecting the presence of encoded blocks, forbidding auto-decoding steps, and logging such requests for human review are effective mitigation strategies.
